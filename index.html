01 How I work

Domain ownership

Insurance is where correctness is not optional. I own coverage selection and versioning, standalone benefits, additional drivers, claims, and the pricing rules that sit underneath them — including the parts nobody wants to touch.

Design before code

Most of my recent work started as a discovery doc: vendor evaluation, data modeling, sequence diagrams, and a written technical design reviewed across teams before a single card was cut.

Measurement built in

Features ship with instrumentation. I've modernized event tracking across the funnel, built experiment infrastructure, and used the resulting data to decide what stayed and what got rolled back.

Release confidence

RSpec, Cypress, and end-to-end coverage on the flows that move money; release checklists, staged rollouts, post-launch monitoring, and the retro afterwards.
